wheresMyT Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

wheresMyT is an unofficial pastime app for entertainment only. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Tesla, Inc. Tesla and Model Y/Model 3 are trademarks of their owner and are used only to describe the vehicle you ordered.

What stays on your device

Everything you enter (your vehicle choices, dates, VIN or reservation number, delivery steps, notes and car photo) is stored only on your device. We do not run a server, we do not have accounts, and we never receive, collect, sell or share this information. The app never asks for your Tesla login and does not connect to your Tesla account; you type in what you see there.

Robotaxi Companion web page

The Robotaxi Companion page (/robotaxi/) keeps its rides, rates and checklist in your browser's local storage on your device. It makes no network requests and sends nothing to us. Clearing site data or using "Delete my data" there removes it.

Network requests the app makes

To show maps, weather, photos and railroad data, the app contacts these public services. Like any website, they can see your device's IP address and the location or place name being requested (for example a town along a route), but not your name, VIN or order details:

  • U.S. Surface Transportation Board (opendata.stb.gov) — railroad averages
  • U.S. Geological Survey (basemap.nationalmap.gov) — satellite imagery
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — weather
  • Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons — town and factory photos
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — map software libraries
  • Apple (itunes.apple.com) — checks whether a newer version of the app is available (the iOS app only)

Links that open Google Maps, YouTube or tesla.com open in your browser and are governed by those sites' own policies.

Sending an idea

The "Ideas & comments" box opens a draft in your own mail app. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself, and then it goes through your email provider. We use your message only to improve the app.

Deleting your data

Use "Delete my data" in the app, or delete the app, to remove everything it stores.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and collects no personal information.
